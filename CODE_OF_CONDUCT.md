# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Aegis community is committed to a respectful, welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information or confidential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, unwanted contact, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberate disruption of project spaces is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation in response to behavior that violates this policy. Report concerns privately through [GitHub contact for opeblow](https://github.com/opeblow); do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
